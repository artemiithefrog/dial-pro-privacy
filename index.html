Privacy Policy

Dial Pro lets you create personal clock faces and display them in Home Screen widgets.

Effective date: 24 October 2026
Developer: Artemii Malyshev

Your clock library stays on your device.

Dial Pro has no account system, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Optional support messages, Apple's TestFlight services, and this website are described below.

1. Data used by the app

Dial Pro stores your clock names, designs, appearance settings, and imported images or fonts locally on your device. These files are used to draw, edit, and display your clocks. The app and its widget share this local library so the widget can display the clock you select.

Dial Pro does not upload your clock library to the developer or operate a server for your content. The app does not collect advertising identifiers or request access to your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

2. Photos and file imports

When you import an image, Dial Pro receives the image you choose through Apple's photo picker. It does not request unrestricted access to your photo library. When you import a clock pack, it reads the files needed from the folder you select in the system file picker and saves copies in its local library.

Importing does not change or delete your original photos or files. If your chosen content is stored with iCloud or another file provider, that provider may download it according to your settings and its own privacy policy.

3. Storage, backups, and deletion

Your saved clocks remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's data. You can delete a clock set from the Dial Pro library. Imported assets used by another saved clock are retained for that clock.

To remove all local Dial Pro data, use Delete App in iOS Settings. Offloading an app keeps its documents and data. Removing a widget alone does not delete your clock library.

Device backups may include app data according to your Apple or computer backup settings. Dial Pro does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Original files in Photos or Files and copies in backups are managed separately through those services.

You can stop importing content at any time and remove saved content as described above. The developer cannot remotely access or delete the clock files on your device.

4. Apple services and TestFlight

Apple may process installation, purchase, and diagnostic information when you use the App Store or Apple's services, under the Apple Privacy Policy. Apple may make diagnostic or aggregate app reports available to the developer according to the service and your settings.

If you use a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares beta-testing information with the developer. This can include the app and OS version, device model, sessions, crashes, and feedback. Your name and email may be available for email invitations; accepting a public invitation link alone does not disclose them to the developer. Feedback can include information and screenshots you choose to submit.

We use TestFlight information to investigate bugs and improve Dial Pro. We do not sell it or share it with third parties. See TestFlight & Privacy for Apple's processing and retention. To stop beta participation, use TestFlight's Stop Testing control. You may contact us about data available to us; Apple also provides its Data and Privacy portal for data it controls.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use this information to reply, resolve your request, and improve the app when you report a problem. Please send only information relevant to your request.

Support email is delivered and stored using Apple iCloud Mail, under Apple's privacy policy. Support correspondence and any copies of beta feedback we retain are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request, investigate the issue, or meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold.

6. This website

This website uses GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts, or tracking scripts to these pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security when serving GitHub Pages sites. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Your choices and requests

Where applicable law provides, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, or object to or request restriction of its use. You can contact us using the email below. You may also contact your local data protection authority. For information stored only on your device, use the local controls in section 3.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support or beta feedback, contact us so we can address it and delete information where appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

If Dial Pro's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Any required notice or permission will be provided before a new use of personal information begins.
