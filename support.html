Add your first widget

  1. Open Dial Pro. Pebble is included as a ready-to-use clock.
  2. Touch and hold the Home Screen, then choose Edit → Add Widget.
  3. Find Dial Pro and add Custom Clock.
  4. Touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget, then select your clock under Clock Set.

Import a clock pack

Save the pack in Files. If it is a ZIP archive, tap it to extract it first. In Dial Pro, tap the import button at the top left and select the entire extracted folder containing manifest.json and its assets. A supported pack creates the available clock appearances together.

You can also create an empty clock set with the + menu and choose your own images in the editor.

Choose a clock for the widget

After importing or creating a clock, touch and hold the Home Screen widget → Edit Widget → Clock Set. Select the new clock. Saving an edit in Dial Pro requests a widget refresh; iOS controls when it appears.

Customize Pebble

Open Pebble and choose an appearance. Change the background or hand colors, drag a selected layer to move it, and pinch to resize it. Use + → Add Pebble to create a fresh copy.

Delete a clock

Touch and hold its card in the Dial Pro library, choose Delete, and confirm. Removing a Home Screen widget leaves the saved clock in the app. See the Privacy Policy for removing all app data and managing backups.

Beta feedback

If you installed Dial Pro through TestFlight, you can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Feedback is optional. Apple's TestFlight service collects beta diagnostics as explained in our Privacy Policy.
